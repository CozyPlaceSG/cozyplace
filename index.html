What the research says
about time together

Researchers who study couples keep finding the same thing: what counts is intentional time together.

“Sharing the same space isn't the same as spending time together.”

The Gottman Institution · 40+ years of data

6 hours a week

The Gottman institution which conducted the longest couple studies with more than 40 years of data found that the less intentional time spend together largely correlates with relationship dissatisfaction. The research further shows that happy couples gave each other about six intentional hours a week.

Most of that is small daily moments.

The takeaway

The biggest contribution is
a three-hour date, just the two of you.

The best solution for lack of intentional time together is shared activities and genuine conversation. No phones, no interruption.

3 shared activities for intentional time in CozyPlace

01

Watching a show together

He let out a small scream at that horror jump scare scene. Then you laugh at him for screaming. Next week, you’re still talking and teasing him about that scene.

A peer-reviewed study shows that binge watching a movie or series together gives couples a shared social connection. The story becomes a unique couple memory that belongs to just the two of you. Like an inside joke.

Months later, you'll still remember that horror movie and those memories.

02

A slow meal without phones and other people

No screens or scrolling halfway through a conversation. No one at the next table. Just the two of you, taking your time.

People bond over food. Genuine conversations, deep or light, happen naturally in a cozy space.

We just need to get rid of the phones, the noise and the disturbance, and let it happen. It's that easy.

03

Couple Card game to know more about one another

How well do you really know one another? Time to find out. Find out what his favourite childhood show was, or her idea of a perfect day. Some answers will make you laugh. Some will surprise you, however long you've been together. How deep you go is up to the both of you.
