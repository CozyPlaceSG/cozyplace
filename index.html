What the research says
about time together

Multiple research on couples shows that what truly matters is spending intentional time together.

“Being in the same space isn't the same as truly connecting”

6 hours a week

Backed by 50 Years of Relationship Research, the longest ever.

The Gottman Institute research shows that less intentional time spent together results in relationship dissatisfaction.

It further shows that happy couples gave each other about six intentional hours a week.

The takeaway

The biggest contribution is a three-hour date, just the two of you.

This means shared activities and genuine conversations. No phones, no interruption.
